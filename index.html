Setup and help

Connect Google Tasks

The app talks to Google directly from your browser. Google needs a one-time "Client ID" that says this app is allowed to ask for your tasks. Paste it here once on each device.

Google lists show up in the sidebar. Untick a list to hide it. Hidden lists are not changed or deleted.

How Apple Reminders gets in

Apple doesn't let websites read Reminders. So an iPhone Shortcut reads them and hands them to this app. It is a copy: tick things off in Apple Reminders, then run the Shortcut again to refresh. When Google is connected, the copy is saved to a Google list so your PC sees it too.

Make the Shortcut (once, on your iPhone)

  1. Open the Shortcuts app, tap +, name it Send Reminders.
  2. Add Find Reminders. Add the filter Is Completed is No.
  3. Add Combine Text: combine Reminders with New Lines.
  4. Add Copy to Clipboard.
  5. Add Open URLs with:
  6. Run it. This app opens with a Paste button. Tap it.

Or paste them in

One reminder per line, as Title|List|Due (List and Due are optional).

Phone

In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. It then opens like an app, straight to the "Add a task" box.

PC

Drag tasks to reorder them or drop them into another list. Click a task's text to rename it. Dragging an Apple reminder into another list makes a copy there.

Where things are saved

Google lists live in your Google account. "Typed here" lists live only in this browser. Once Google is connected, add to a Google list so it shows on both phone and PC.
